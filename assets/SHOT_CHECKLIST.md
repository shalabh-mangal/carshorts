# 12-shot checklist — film once per car, feeds every future video

Phone settings: 4K, 30 or 60fps, VERTICAL, lens wiped, exposure locked on the
car (tap-hold). Move SLOWLY — half your walking speed. 5-8s per shot.
AirDrop the ORIGINAL files into assets/inbox/ (never screen-record).

EXTERIOR (park somewhere clean; keep the number plate out of frame or masked)
 1. Slow orbit: front 3/4 -> side, knee height
 2. Low front hero: crouch, phone near ground, tilt up slightly
 3. Rear 3/4 sweep (skip if plate unavoidable — I can blur, clean is better)
 4. Wheel + brake detail: slow arc around one wheel
 5. Badge macro: logo fills the frame, slow push-in
 6. Headlamp/DRL detail: catch a reflection moving across it

INTERIOR (doors open for light; screens ON)
 7. Dashboard pan: driver door -> across wheel -> touchscreen
 8. Touchscreen boot/menu swipe (finger in frame is good)
 9. Instrument cluster close-up, needle sweep / display animation
10. AC vents + climate controls detail
11. Seat/space shot: rear legroom or boot (empty + tidy!)

ACTION (only if safely possible; passenger films, never the driver)
12. Rolling shot: car passes camera slowly / driving POV over dashboard

Nice-to-have: 4x4 shifter close-up, sunroof, key fob in hand, fuel cap.
