## New learnings
- Keep using question-based hooks: the Kia Sonet video with a question hook got 1557 views, while the Creta news hook got only 244. Also, the Swift deadpan question hook had 478 views with high retention (130% avg_view_pct), suggesting questions engage viewers.
- Shorten the spec section: multiple videos (Sonet, Scorpio-N, Thar, Punch) show the worst retention drop at the 'spec' beat. For example, Sonet dropped 64.9% at spec, Scorpio-N dropped 54.1%. Trim spec details or present them more visually to maintain interest.
- Avoid using overly dark or blurry stock footage: QA failures on Tata Sierra and Brezza show too_dark_or_blurry issues. For renderer: filter out low-quality clips before final render. For script writer: avoid phrases that might mismatch with available clips.
- Ensure no clip reuse: Tata Sierra failed QA due to repeated clips (sierra_sunroof, sierra_dash). For renderer: implement a check to prevent reuse of the same clip across cuts, or expand the stock footage pool.
- The Brezza video with only 40 views shows weak signal, but its retention curve dropped sharply after the first 20%, possibly due to the hype persona. For script writer: consider testing more deadpan or default personas for consistency, as the Sonet deadpan with question hook performed better.
