While preparing and iterating **draft** versions, use **free tools** (e.g. edge-tts for voice, free LLM tiers, free stock/stills). Only switch to **premium/paid tools** (ElevenLabs, paid AI-video, etc.) for the **final, confirmed output** — never for drafts or comparisons.

**Why:** premium credits are scarce (ElevenLabs free tier ≈ 10k chars/month) and burning them on drafts wastes them. Voice/quality upgrades are one-line swaps at the seam, so there's no reason to pay early.

**How to apply:** default every iteration to the free path; when the user confirms a script/video is final, then (and only then) render the final with the premium tool. Pairs with [[feedback-confirm-costly-scarce]] (ask before spending any scarce/costly resource).
